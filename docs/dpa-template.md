# Data Processing Agreement (Template)

> **Legal review required.** This template is a starting point for the
> university's counsel and is not legal advice.

## Parties and purpose

The university (the **Controller**) appoints the portal operator (the
**Processor**) to host and operate the Novastra Student Portal for student,
faculty, and administrative workflows.

## Processing instructions

Processing is limited to authentication, academic records, fees, support
requests, notifications, audit/security logs, and backups. The Processor must
not sell data, use it for advertising, or process it outside documented
instructions.

## Security and confidentiality

The Processor maintains least-privilege access, tenant isolation, encryption in
transit, HTTP-only cookies, audit logging, rate limiting, vulnerability
management, and tested backup/restore procedures. Personnel with access are
bound by confidentiality.

## Sub-processors and transfers

SMTP, object storage, payment, monitoring, and hosting providers must be
listed in an approved sub-processor register. New sub-processors require
notice and approval. International transfers require the university's legal
transfer mechanism and documented safeguards.

## Incidents and assistance

The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay after discovering
a personal-data breach and will provide relevant facts, containment actions,
and remediation updates. The Processor will assist with access, correction,
deletion, retention, and regulatory requests.

## Retention and deletion

At termination, the Processor returns or securely deletes university data and
certifies deletion, except for legally required retention. Retention schedules
and restore copies must be documented by the Controller.

## Audit and sign-off

The Controller may review security evidence and commission an external-only
penetration test against an approved staging target. Complete party details,
processing categories, retention periods, sub-processors, governing law, and
signatures before production use.
