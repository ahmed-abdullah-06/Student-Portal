# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Novastra University (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you access and use the Novastra Student Portal (the “Service”).

By using the Service, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Account credentials (email, hashed password)
- Profile information (name, contact details, profile picture)
- Academic records (enrollment, grades, attendance, course information)
- Financial information (fee payment details, challan information)
- Communication records (messages, complaints, requests)

### Automatic Information
- Log data (IP address, browser type, device information)
- Usage data (pages visited, features used, time spent)
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies

## How We Use Your Information

1. **Service Provision**: To provide access to the portal, manage your account, and deliver university services
2. **Communication**: To send notifications, updates, and important information
3. **Security**: To protect against unauthorized access and ensure system integrity
4. **Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and enhance the Service
5. **Compliance**: To meet legal and regulatory requirements

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage
- Your personal data is stored in PostgreSQL databases
- Access is controlled through tenant-based security
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest where possible

### Security Measures
- Role-based access controls
- Session management with token revocation
- Regular security audits
- Monitoring and alerting for suspicious activity

## Your Rights

### Data Access and Control
- Access your personal data through the portal
- Update or correct your information
- Request export of your data in a portable format
- Request deletion of your account and data

### Opt-Out
- You may opt out of non-essential communications
- You may disable certain data collection through account settings

### Data Deletion
- When you delete your account, your personal data will be deleted within 30 days
- Certain data may be retained for legal or operational reasons (audit logs, financial records)

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Maintain your session
- Remember your preferences
- Provide personalized experiences
- Analyze Service usage

You can control cookie usage through your browser settings.

## Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help operate the Service:
- Email delivery services
- Analytics providers
- Storage solutions

These services have their own privacy policies and are bound by contract to protect your data.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

## Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of significant changes.
